CrossPlan

Your personal command center for email, calendar, and tasks — unified in one workspace, on every Apple device.

CrossPlan is a native macOS, iPadOS, and iOS application that brings together the parts of daily life that usually live in separate apps. Multiple email accounts across Gmail and Microsoft 365. Every calendar you've subscribed to on your device. Your system reminders alongside CrossPlan's own task manager. One workspace, one keyboard shortcut to anything.

Built with the same principles as every Longevity Health Analytics app: your data stays on your device. No servers. No analytics. No tracking. Connections to Gmail and Microsoft 365 happen directly from your machine; tasks sync only through your personal iCloud. CrossPlan is private by construction.

📧

Unified Inbox

Read, reply, archive, and search across every connected mailbox — Gmail and Microsoft 365 — in a single Gmail-style threaded view, color-coded by account.

📅

Calendar at a Glance

Day and week views over every calendar your device knows about — iCloud, Google, Microsoft Exchange, ICS subscriptions, family calendars. Create and edit events with the right recurrence semantics.

✅

Tasks That Add Up

System reminders from every source (iCloud, Exchange, On My Mac) merge with CrossPlan's own task manager. Smart lists for Today, This Week, Overdue, and Later. Categories, priorities, subtasks.

🔒

Private by Construction

OAuth tokens stored in the system Keychain. Email cached locally only, never synced. Tasks live in your personal iCloud — partitioned per profile. No telemetry, no third-party trackers, no ads.

👥

Built for Two

Designed for couples and individuals managing multiple identities — separate profiles with isolated email, tasks, and categories on the same Mac. A shared family calendar visible to both.

🧠

Claude-Powered Triage

Coming soon: opt-in AI assistance for inbox triage, reply drafting, and a daily briefing — powered by Anthropic's Claude API. Off by default; on only when you flip the switch.

Availability

CrossPlan is currently in active development as a personal tool. It is not yet available on the App Store. This page exists so the underlying email integrations have a public home for their OAuth consent flows.
